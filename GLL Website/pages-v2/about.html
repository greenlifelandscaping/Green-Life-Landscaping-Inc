Our Story

20+ Years of Showing Up,
Doing the Work Right.

Green Life Landscaping started in 2005 with a single truck, a small crew, and one principle: show up when you say you will, and do the work right. That hasn't changed.

What started as a handful of neighborhood lawn-care clients in Burtonsville has grown into a full-service outdoor company serving residential and commercial properties across Montgomery County. We've never grown so fast that we lost the basics — same crew, same trucks, same standard year after year.

Today, we handle everything from weekly mowing and seasonal cleanups to full hardscape installations, drainage systems, and tree work. One trusted team. One honest contract. No surprises.
