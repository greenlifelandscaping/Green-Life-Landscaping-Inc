What's Included

Lawn Care Done the
Right Way.

A healthy Maryland lawn takes more than a weekly cut. We pair consistent mowing with the seasonal services — aeration, overseeding, fertilization, and lime treatments — that build long-term turf health.

For most Montgomery County lawns, we recommend weekly mowing April through October paired with two to three deep-fertilization treatments per year. We adapt frequency to grass type, growth rate, sun exposure, and your goals.

Services Included

  • Weekly & Bi-Weekly Mowing
    Sharp blade cuts, rotated mow patterns, edges & trimmed every visit. We blow off all hard surfaces before leaving.
  • Core Aeration
    Spring or fall deep-soil aeration to relieve compaction and improve root depth — pairs with overseeding for best results.
  • Overseeding & Slit-Seeding
    Cool-season grass seed (turf-type tall fescue, KBG blends) for thicker, more durable Maryland turf.
  • Fertilization Programs
    3–5 application annual programs with slow-release nitrogen, pre-emergent crabgrass control, and grub treatments as needed.
  • Dethatching & Lawn Renovation
    Power dethatching, scalping, and full lawn renovations for properties past the point of just-mowing.
  • Edging & Detail Trim Work
    Crisp bed edges, trimmed fence lines, and string-trimmed obstacles every visit. Looks pro every week.
