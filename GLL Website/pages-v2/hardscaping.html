What's Included

Hardscaping Done the
Right Way.

Most hardscape failures come down to one thing: bad base. We over-prep every install — proper excavation, geotextile fabric, and compacted aggregate — so what we build still looks right a decade later.

Every hardscape project starts with a written estimate, material spec sheet, and clear timeline. Most patios and walls complete in 1–2 weeks. Larger multi-zone projects run 3–4 weeks.

Services Included

  • Paver Patios
    Concrete pavers, natural flagstone, and travertine patios installed over compacted base with proper drainage slope.
  • Retaining Walls
    Segmental retaining walls (SRW), dry-stack stone, and timber walls — engineered for the load they hold.
  • Walkways & Paths
    Front-entry walkways, side paths, and connecting paths in pavers, flagstone, or pea gravel.
  • Fire Pits & Outdoor Living
    Fire pits, seat walls, pergola pads, and outdoor kitchen prep. Pair with patio installs for one cohesive build.
  • Steps & Risers
    Stone steps, paver risers, and grade transitions built to code and to last.
  • Driveways
    Permeable paver and traditional concrete-paver driveways. Higher curb appeal than asphalt, longer life than concrete.
