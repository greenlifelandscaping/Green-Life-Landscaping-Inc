Lawn Care Services We Provide

Lawn Mowing

Weekly or bi-weekly mowing at the correct height for your grass type, with clean edging along walkways, driveways, and beds. We leave your lawn looking groomed — not just cut.

Aeration & Overseeding

Core aeration opens compacted soil to let water, nutrients, and air reach the roots. Paired with overseeding, it fills in thin or bare spots and thickens your lawn over time. Best done in fall for Maryland lawns.

Fertilization Programs

Multi-step seasonal fertilization programs that feed your lawn at the right times. We use the appropriate formulas for cool-season grasses common in Maryland to promote deep roots and dense growth.

Dethatching

Thatch buildup blocks water and nutrients from reaching the soil. We mechanically remove the thatch layer to restore healthy airflow and improve the effectiveness of watering and fertilization.

Seasonal Cleanups

Spring and fall cleanups remove leaf debris, dead growth, and winter damage. A proper cleanup at the start and end of each season sets your lawn up for healthier growth.

Weed & Edge Control

Clean bed edges and targeted weed control keep your lawn looking defined and polished. We address weeds as part of ongoing maintenance — not just as a one-time add-on.

Why Consistent Lawn Care Matters

Many homeowners underestimate how much regular maintenance impacts lawn health. Mowing at the wrong height, skipping fertilization, or neglecting aeration all contribute to thinning, yellowing, and weeds. The fix is usually simple — but it requires showing up on schedule.

That's our job. When you work with Green Life Landscaping, you get a consistent crew who knows your lawn and shows up as scheduled — not when it's convenient. Over time, that consistency is what produces the results you're looking for.
