When Should You Remove a Tree?

Not every tree needs to come down — but some should. Here are common signs that a tree on your Maryland property may need professional attention:

  • The tree is dead or more than 50% diseased
  • Large dead branches ("widow makers") are hanging over your home, driveway, or yard
  • The tree is leaning significantly or the root system is visibly compromised
  • The tree has suffered major storm damage and structural integrity is in question
  • Branches are growing into power lines, your roof, or neighboring structures
  • You're doing construction or landscaping work and the tree is in the way
