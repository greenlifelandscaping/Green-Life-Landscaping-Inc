Theme & builder

Make it look like
our site.

⏱ ~ 30 min · One-time

Out of the box WordPress looks like a 2014 blog. The theme controls the global look (header style, body font, default colors). The page builder is the drag-and-drop tool you use to lay out each page. We're using Kadence theme + Kadence Blocks together — they're built by the same team, free, fast, and the closest match to the visual language of our prototype.

1

Install the Kadence theme

From your dashboard: Appearance → Themes → Add New. In the search box type Kadence. The first result is the one. Click Install, then Activate.

Install Kadence Blocks (the page builder)

Now go to Plugins → Add New, search Kadence Blocks. Same drill — Install, then Activate. This adds 30+ drag-and-drop blocks (rows, columns, image cards, accordions, testimonials) to the WordPress editor.

Run the Kadence Starter Wizard

4

Go to Appearance → Customize → Colors. Replace the defaults with our brand palette. Then under Typography, set Headings = Montserrat (weight 800) and Body = Montserrat (weight 400). These cascade across every page you build.

  • Text: #1a1a1a
  • Background: #ffffff

Global setup

Logo, header,
footer, mega menu.

⏱ ~ 2 hours · One-time

Every page on the site shares the same header, footer and global styling. Set these up before building any pages — fix the chrome once, instead of fixing it on every page.

Upload your logo

Appearance → Customize → Header → Site Identity. Upload logo-primary.png. Set "Logo Width" to 180px. Tick "Display Site Title" off — your logo replaces the text.

2

Build the navigation menu

Appearance → Menus → Create a new menu. Call it "Main." Add these items in order: Home, About, Services, Gallery, Contact. Save. In Display Location, tick "Primary Menu."

3

Turn the Services item into a mega menu

(Kadence Pro only — skip if you're staying free; you'll get a normal dropdown which is still fine.)

In Appearance → Menus, click the Services item to expand it. Tick "Mega Menu." A new layout panel appears. Drag in five sub-items (Landscape Design, Hardscaping, Lawn Care, Tree Service, Snow Removal) plus an "All Services" link at the bottom. For each sub-item, click the icon picker and choose a Lucide icon. Add a one-line description.

?

06
SEO & local search

Get found by
your neighbors.

⏱ ~ 1 hour

You're a local landscaper. Your job isn't to rank for "landscaping" — it's to rank for "landscaping in Burtonsville," "hardscape contractor Montgomery County MD," "snow removal near Silver Spring." That's local SEO and Rank Math handles 90% of it for free.

1

Run the Rank Math setup wizard

The first time you open Rank Math it walks you through: site name, business type (pick "Local Business → Landscaper"), business address, hours, phone, social profiles. This data becomes the structured "schema" Google reads to show your business in the local 3-pack.

2

Connect Google Search Console

Rank Math → Dashboard → "Connect to Google." Sign in. This lets Google know your site exists and tells you what people are searching when they find you. Submit your sitemap: the URL Rank Math gives you (something like greenlifelandscaping.com/sitemap_index.xml) goes into Search Console under "Sitemaps."

3

Write a meta title & description for every page

At the bottom of each page editor you'll now see a Rank Math panel. Fill in two things per page:

  • Meta title (~55 chars). Pattern: Page topic | GreenLife Landscaping Burtonsville MD
  • Meta description (~155 chars). One honest sentence about what's on the page + your phone number.
4

Claim your Google Business Profile

Outside WordPress: go to google.com/business and verify ownership of GreenLife Landscaping. Fill in the same hours, phone, photos. Get your first 5 reviews from past customers — that's the single biggest local-search lever.

5

Sprinkle your service area in the copy

On each service page, mention Burtonsville and 2 — 3 nearby towns naturally — "we serve Burtonsville, Silver Spring, Olney and the rest of Montgomery County." Don't stuff it; once per page is plenty. Google's reading it; humans are skimming for trust.

✓
The 80/20 of local SEOMost of your traffic will come from Google Maps, not the search results. Spend more time getting reviews on your Google Business Profile than tweaking meta tags. Five real reviews beat fifty pages of content for a local contractor.

07
Performance, security & launch

Make it fast.
Make it safe.
Go live.

⏱ ~ 1 hour

Performance

1

Turn on caching

Go to LiteSpeed Cache → General. Click "Enable LiteSpeed Cache: ON." That's it for caching — your pages now load from a stored copy instead of being rebuilt on every visit.

2

Compress your images

ShortPixel → Bulk → "Optimize all." It'll chew through your media library, converting JPGs to WebP and trimming file sizes. Your gallery page will load 3× faster after this.

3

Run a speed check

Open pagespeed.web.dev and paste your homepage URL. Aim for green (90+) on mobile. If you're below 70, the most likely culprit is unoptimized hero images — re-export them at 1920px wide, 80% JPEG quality.

Security & backups

4

Configure Wordfence

Wordfence → Login Security → Set 2-factor auth on your admin account. Wordfence → Firewall → "Optimize the Wordfence Firewall." Done — you're now blocking 99% of automated attacks.

5

Schedule weekly backups

UpdraftPlus → Settings → Files = weekly, Database = daily. Storage = Google Drive (it'll walk you through linking it). Click "Backup Now" once for an initial snapshot. Sleep better.

Launch day

6

Final walkthrough

Open every page on your phone. Tap every button. Submit the contact form. Click every gallery filter. Open the mega menu. Anything broken? Fix it now, before the URL is public.

7

Make the site public

Settings → Reading → uncheck "Discourage search engines from indexing this site." This was on by default while you built — turn it off only at launch.

8

Tell the world

Update Google Business Profile with your new URL. Add the URL to your truck signs, business cards, email signature, Facebook page. The website is the hub; everywhere you already exist points to it.
